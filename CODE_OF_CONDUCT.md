# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful outcomes directly.
- Protect the privacy of users, contributors, and data subjects.
- Keep personal information out of public reports and development artifacts.
- Accept maintainer decisions about safety, legality, and project scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, publication of personal
information, credential misuse, or attempts to use this project against an
unauthorized person are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
material that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Sensitive conduct reports should use a private channel rather
than a public issue.
