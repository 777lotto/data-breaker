# Responsible use

Use `data-breaker` only to exercise privacy choices for:

- yourself;
- another person who has explicitly authorized you; or
- a person for whom you have valid legal authority to act.

## Required conduct

- Provide truthful identity, residency, relationship, and authorization
  information.
- Review the destination and requested fields before submission.
- Respect broker-specific eligibility and verification requirements.
- Allow the subject to complete login, MFA, identity, or residency checks when
  appropriate.
- Preserve authorization evidence without publishing it.
- Comply with applicable laws and site terms.

## Prohibited conduct

Do not use this project to:

- submit requests for an unrelated or nonconsenting person;
- access another person's account or mailbox without permission;
- impersonate a consumer or authorized agent;
- bypass CAPTCHAs, MFA, access controls, or rate limits;
- flood, harass, probe, or disrupt a service;
- upload false documents or make false legal claims;
- collect, republish, or enrich brokered personal information.

`data-breaker` is a technical tool, not legal advice. Availability of a form does
not establish a legal right, and a submitted request does not prove deletion.
