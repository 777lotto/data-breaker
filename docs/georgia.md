# Georgia jurisdiction note

Status checked: 2026-08-20.

Georgia (`US-GA`) is the first development jurisdiction for `data-breaker`.

## SB 111 warning

SB 111 was introduced in 2025 with the title “Georgia Consumer Privacy
Protection Act.” During the 2026 session, the bill was substituted. The final
enacted text amends the rural-hospital tax-credit definition and does not enact
the introduced consumer privacy rights.

Primary sources:

- [Final enrolled SB 111/AP](https://www.legis.ga.gov/api/legislation/document/20252026/249143)
- [Governor's description of signed SB 111](https://gov.georgia.gov/press-releases/2026-05-11/gov-kemp-signs-legislation-lowering-taxes-and-supporting-economic-growth)

Some bill indexes retain the introduced title. Adapters must use the final
enacted text, not stale bill metadata.

## Initial routing policy

Georgia adapters may use:

- published removal or suppression processes open to Georgia residents;
- nationwide privacy programs voluntarily offered by a broker;
- another verified legal basis that actually applies to the data and request.

They must not claim:

- California residency or DROP eligibility;
- GDPR or another jurisdiction's rights;
- a deletion right under the introduced version of Georgia SB 111.

The initial live-broker survey will classify routes by policy basis and verify
each one before implementation.
